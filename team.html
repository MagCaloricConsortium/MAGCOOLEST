Team

Three groups with non-overlapping expertise: fundamental mechanisms and modelling in Oviedo, processing and scale-up in the Basque Country, device design and validation at BCMaterials.

University of Oviedo — subproject 1

Coordinating subproject. Reference PID2025-174502NB-C31.

Pablo Álvarez Alonso — principal investigator, project coordinator
Overall coordination, synthesis of polycrystalline alloys and discrete composites, multicaloric characterisation.
Pedro Gorria Korres — principal investigator
Effect of mechanical stress on caloric mechanisms, powdering, magnetic characterisation, data management.
Jesús A. Blanco
Thermal characterisation, synchrotron radiation experiments, high magnetic field measurements.
Pablo Nieves
Computational pre-screening, compositionally modulated design, Monte Carlo and magnetoelastic calculations.

Collaborating researchers

José L. Sánchez Llamazares — IPICYT, Mexico

Gabriel J. Cuello — Institut Laue-Langevin, France

Anipinder Singh — Guru Nanak Dev University, India
Design and validation of energy harvesting demonstrators.
Hariharan Srikanth — University of South Florida, USA
Magnetic characterisation and transverse susceptometry.
