30 September 2026

Kick-off meeting sets the working rules

The three subprojects met to agree how the consortium will work day to day. Computational pre-screening starts first, so that synthesis is guided rather than exploratory. The groups will keep a shared inventory of equipment to avoid buying twice what one laboratory already has. Responsibility for open-access charges rotates between groups, independently of who leads a given paper. All data go to a single Zenodo community rather than three separate ones.

September 2026

MAGCOOLEST begins

The project is funded by the Spanish State Research Agency under the 2025 Knowledge Generation call, as a coordinated project between the University of Oviedo, the University of the Basque Country and BCMaterials, running for three years.

Funded by MICIU/AEI, grants PID2025-174502NB-C31, PID2025-174502NB-C32 and PID2025-174502NB-C33, co-funded by the European Union.
