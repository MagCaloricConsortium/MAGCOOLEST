Open science

Measurements, simulations and analyses produced by the three subprojects are deposited in a single public repository, documented so that someone outside the consortium can reuse them.

Data

All datasets are deposited in the MAGCOOLEST community on Zenodo under a CC BY 4.0 licence, with the grant reference recorded in the funding field of every deposit. Each dataset carries metadata describing the sample, its composition, the synthesis route, the measurement technique and conditions, the calibration protocol, the responsible group and a contact person.

Data are stored in open formats wherever possible: plain text for numerical data, CSV for tables, TIFF or JPEG for images, and documented software-specific formats for computational outputs.
